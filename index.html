Cosmos Google Access

A private tool operated by Knut Wien, MultiPitch AS, Norway.

HomePrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

What it is

Cosmos Google Access lets Knut Wien's own software assistants work with Knut Wien's own Google accounts: read and organise e-mail, manage calendar events, work with files in Google Drive and look up contacts. It runs only on computers Knut Wien owns.

Who can use it

Only Knut Wien, for Google accounts he owns. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

How access works

Access is granted by Knut Wien through Google's own consent screen, one account at a time, and can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
